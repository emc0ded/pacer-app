Pacer

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Pacer: Running Companion ("Pacer", "we", "our") is a run tracking app built and maintained by Emily Herbert. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how it is stored.

Data We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to make the app work:

  • Account information — your name and email address, provided when you sign in with Apple or Google.
  • Precise location — GPS coordinates recorded during a run to calculate your route, distance, and pace. Location is only accessed while the app is in use.
  • Run data — distance, duration, pace, and route coordinates for each run you log.

How We Use Your Data

All data is used solely to provide app functionality:

  • Your account information identifies your data in our database so it syncs across devices.
  • Location data is used in real time to track your run and is stored as part of your run history.
  • Run data is displayed in your history, stats, and progress toward weekly goals.

We do not use your data for advertising, analytics, or any purpose beyond running the app.

Third-Party Services

Pacer uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • Firebase (Google) — stores your account and run data securely in the cloud. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Sign in with Apple / Google Sign-In — used for authentication only. We do not receive your password.
  • Apple HealthKit — with your permission, Pacer writes completed runs to Apple Health as workouts. We do not read any health data from HealthKit.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with any third parties beyond the services listed above that are required to operate the app.

Data Retention

Your data is stored as long as you have an account. You can delete your runs individually within the app. To request full account deletion, contact us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

Pacer is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
